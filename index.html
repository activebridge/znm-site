Honest trade-offs

What you give up.

Zepp doesn’t let other apps change its notifications, so ZNM works around it. Mark-as-read alone changes nothing on your watch. The extras come with a catch, and you should know it before you switch them on.

With Notify+

Your messages move into Notify+

  • Zepp’s own notification list stays empty for the apps you move over. They live in the Notify+ inbox instead.
  • Needs your phone nearby with the Zepp app running. If Notify+ loses the connection, ZNM falls back to regular Zepp notifications, so nothing is missed.
ZNM only · Deliver

Zepp’s screen, ZNM’s copy

  • The ZNM icon instead of Telegram’s. Android doesn’t let one app post as another; the app’s name or emoji stays in the title.
  • Buttons are a workaround. Zepp shows no real buttons, so actions go through Reply: a letter or a quick response like 🗄 archives. It works, but takes a few taps.
  • Starred contacts stop vibrating differently. Zepp offers them only for Telegram and WhatsApp themselves.

Change your mind? Switch Deliver off for an app, turn it back on in Zepp, and everything is as it was.

FAQ

Questions.

Why does ZNM need notification access?

It’s the only way Android lets an app notice when your watch clears a message. ZNM watches for that one moment and marks the chat read. For apps you don’t switch to Deliver, it doesn’t read message text at all.

Will my phone behave differently?

No. If you swipe a notification away on the phone, ZNM leaves it alone on purpose. Only a delete on the watch marks it read.

Which apps work?

Any app that offers a “Mark as read” action, like Telegram, WhatsApp, Signal and Messages. Apps without one can’t be marked read from a delete.

Why does my watch show the ZNM icon?

With Deliver on, ZNM posts the messages to your watch, and Zepp shows the icon of the app that posted them. Android doesn’t let one app post as another, so the app’s name or emoji stays in the title instead. Notify+ shows the real app icon.

What happens when the trial ends?

Messages keep arriving. Delete-to-read, one entry per chat and clean text stay free forever. Notify+, link previews, app emoji and watch commands need Pro, a single $9.99 payment.

Why is Zepp’s notification list empty?

Apps you move to Notify+ arrive in the Notify+ inbox instead of Zepp’s list, so there’s one place to read and clear them. If Notify+ isn’t connected, ZNM sends them through Zepp as usual.

Is there an iPhone version?

No. On iPhone, only the watch maker’s own app (Zepp) can receive other apps’ notifications, and only in the EU, so an app like ZNM can’t work there.
